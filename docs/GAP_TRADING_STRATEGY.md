# Gap Trading Strategy Guide
**Strategy**: Extended Hours Gap Discovery → Regular Hours Exploitation  
**Focus**: Day-0 Gap Momentum Capture (Academic Research-Based)  
**Risk Level**: Medium to High (with proper risk management)  
**Hold Time**: Gap day ONLY - no overnight holds

## Executive Summary

**What**: Day-0 gap trading strategy targeting stocks with ≥2% price gaps and strong volume confirmation.

**When**: DISCOVER gaps during extended hours (4:00 AM - 9:30 AM ET), EXECUTE during regular hours (9:30 AM - 11:30 AM ET), EXIT by market close.

**Why**: 90-year academic study shows 61.8% win rate, but gap momentum dissipates completely by day +1.

**Core Timing Arbitrage**: Information discovered in illiquid extended hours, exploited during liquid regular hours for optimal execution and institutional flow alignment.

⚠️ **Reality Check**: Academic research shows stock gap trading has significant limitations. Market efficiency theory suggests limited exploitable opportunities (only FOREX gaps show consistent 60%+ win rates). Gap fill myth debunked - only 20% fill within 5 days, not 80-90% as commonly believed.

**Key Rules:**
- Gap discovery during extended hours (4:00 AM - 9:30 AM ET)
- Gap size ≥2% with ≥2x volume confirmation
- Execution ONLY during regular hours (9:30 AM - 11:30 AM ET)
- Same-day exit mandatory (academic research requirement)
- Maximum 2% position size with tight stops
- Focus on news-catalyst gaps with institutional participation

## Risk Management Framework

### Market Regime-Based Position Sizing

**Dynamic Position Sizing Algorithm:**

```python
def calculate_position_size(gap_percent, volume_ratio, catalyst_score, volatility):
    base_size = 0.02  # 2% baseline
    gap_multiplier = min(gap_percent / 2.0, 1.5)  # Larger gaps get more size
    volume_multiplier = min(volume_ratio / 2.0, 1.2)  # Higher volume gets more
    catalyst_multiplier = catalyst_score / 100
    volatility_penalty = max(0.5, 1 - (volatility - 20) / 100)  # Reduce in high VIX
    
    return base_size * gap_multiplier * volume_multiplier * catalyst_multiplier * volatility_penalty
```

**Market Regime Adjustments:**

```
Bull Market (S&P up >1% week): Long bias, 3% max position size
Bear Market (S&P down >1% week): Short bias, 1.5% max position size  
Sideways (<1% weekly movement): Pause strategy or 1% max size
High VIX (>25): Reduce all position sizes by 50%
```

### Position Sizing Rules

**Conservative Approach:**
- Maximum 2% of portfolio per individual trade
- Aggregate gap exposure limit: 6% maximum total
- Reduce to 1% for gaps <2% or catalyst score <70
- Increase to 3% only for highest conviction setups (catalyst score >85 + volume >3x + sector momentum)
- Never risk more than you can afford to lose on any single trade

**Account Size Guidelines:**
- $25K account: Maximum $500 per trade
- $50K account: Maximum $1,000 per trade
- $100K account: Maximum $2,000 per trade
- $100K+ account: Maximum 2% of account value

### Stop-Loss Strategy

**Dynamic Stop Placement:**

**ATR-Based Stops (Recommended):**
- Calculate 14-day Average True Range (ATR)
- Set stop at 1.5x ATR from entry price
- Adjusts automatically for volatility
- More scientific than fixed percentages

**Traditional Stop Options:**
- Below/above the gap fill level (but monitor for quick fills)
- At pre-market low/high levels
- At previous day's close (gap fill level)
- At nearest significant technical support/resistance

**Stop-Loss Implementation Rules:**
- Set stop-loss immediately upon entry
- Maximum 2% loss from entry point
- Consider 1% stop for smaller accounts or high volatility
- Never move stops against you ("hoping it comes back")
- Use trailing stops for profitable positions after 1:1 risk/reward achieved

### Time-Based Exits (Academic Research-Based)

**Precise Exit Protocol:**

**Day-0 Only Rule (Critical):**
- ALL POSITIONS MUST CLOSE BY 3:45 PM ET on gap day
- Academic research shows momentum effect dissipates by day +1
- NO OVERNIGHT HOLDS - violates core academic finding

**Intraday Exit Timing:**
- Target exit within 2-4 hours maximum
- Many gaps show final direction in first 30-60 minutes
- If profitable at 3:45 PM but showing weakness: EXIT immediately
- If at stop-loss level near close: EXIT regardless of day P&L

**Time-Based Exit Rules:**
```
9:30-11:30 AM: Primary execution and momentum capture window
11:30 AM-3:30 PM: Position management and profit-taking
3:30-3:45 PM: Prepare for mandatory exit
3:45-4:00 PM: Final exit window - close ALL positions
```

### Take Profit Strategy

**Scale-Out Approach:**
- Take 33% profits at 1:1 risk/reward ratio
- Take another 33% at 1.5:1 risk/reward ratio
- Let final 33% run to 2:1 or 3:1 with trailing stop
- Don't get greedy - quick profits compound over time
- Better to leave money on the table than give back gains

**Profit Target Guidelines:**
- In first hour: Target quick 1:1 profits on 50% of position
- Strong momentum: Let winners run with trailing stops
- Weak momentum: Take profits early and reduce risk
- Near close (3:30 PM+): Take any profitable exit available

### Market Condition Adjustments

**Bull Market Optimization:**
- Focus more on long gaps (upward bias)
- Higher success rate for upward momentum
- Can afford slightly larger position sizes (up to 3%)
- Market provides tailwind for momentum strategies

**Bear Market Adaptation:**
- Focus more on short gaps (downward bias)
- Be more selective with long positions
- Reduce overall position sizes by 25-50%
- Quicker profit-taking recommended (1:1 vs 2:1 targets)

**Sideways/Choppy Market Strategy:**
- Most challenging environment for gap trading
- Reduce position sizes significantly (1% maximum)
- Focus only on highest-conviction setups (score 85+)
- Consider stepping aside entirely during prolonged chop

**High Volatility Periods (VIX >25):**
- Reduce all position sizes by 50%
- Tighten stop-losses (1.5% vs 2%)
- Take profits more quickly (1:1 vs 1.5:1)
- Increase quality threshold (score 75+ vs 60+)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Emotional Traps

**FOMO (Fear of Missing Out):**
- Chasing gaps that have already moved significantly
- Entering without proper setup confirmation
- Ignoring risk management rules when "sure thing" appears

**Revenge Trading:**
- Doubling down after a loss
- Abandoning strategy after bad trade
- Taking excessive risk to "get even"

**Overconfidence:**
- Increasing position sizes after wins
- Ignoring stop-losses on "can't lose" trades
- Trading every gap instead of waiting for quality

### Technical Mistakes

**Poor Entry Timing:**
- Entering at market open without confirmation
- Buying into resistance or selling into support
- Ignoring overall market tape

**Inadequate Research:**
- Not verifying news catalyst quality
- Ignoring volume confirmation
- Missing sector/market context

**Risk Management Failures:**
- Position sizes too large for account
- No stop-loss or moving stops against position
- Holding losing trades too long
